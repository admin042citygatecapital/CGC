## General Steps for Any Jurisdiction

1. **Identify the regulatory body** — Which government/authority regulates fintech/payments in your jurisdiction?
2. **Classify your business model** — Are you a money transmitter? E-money issuer? Payment processor? Investment platform?
3. **Obtain licensing** — Apply for the appropriate license or exemption.
4. **Implement compliance framework** — KYC/AML, transaction monitoring, reporting.
5. **Establish banking partnerships** — Secure liquidity and settlement accounts.
6. **Set up regulatory reporting** — Quarterly or annual filings as required.
7. **Maintain audit trails** — Immutable logs of all transactions and compliance decisions.
8. **Conduct external audit** — Engage a third party to verify compliance annually.
