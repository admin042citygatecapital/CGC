# Google Business Profile verification

Status: **blocked pending owner evidence and Google verification**

The proposed location is Citygate, 51 Mosley Street, Manchester, M2 3HQ. The building and postcode are real, but the current Google street-level result identifies Barclays Bank. The active UK company with the matching name, CITYGATE CAPITAL LIMITED (company 11575573), currently lists a different registered office at Companies House. A registered office can differ from an operating office, but neither item proves that City Gate Capital occupies or may publicly represent the Manchester address.

## Evidence required before publication

- A signed lease, licence to occupy, or landlord confirmation naming the correct legal entity.
- A recent utility bill, business-rates record, or equivalent document matching the entity and address.
- Permanent City Gate Capital signage at the premises.
- Staff present during the hours published on Google.
- Confirmation that customers can meet the business at this location.
- The responsible legal entity name and Companies House number.
- Written approval from an owner or authorised representative to publish the address.

Do not place identity documents, account details, access codes, or confidential lease terms in the website admin note. Record only a short evidence reference and retain the source documents in an access-controlled compliance system.

## Owner verification steps

1. Sign in to the Google account that will own the Business Profile.
2. Add or claim City Gate Capital using the exact real-world business name.
3. Enter the address only if the location meets Google's staffed-location and signage rules.
4. Use the verification method Google offers; it cannot be selected manually.
5. If video verification is offered, record one uninterrupted live video showing the street/building, permanent business signage, and proof of authorised management access.
6. After Google approves the profile, record the evidence reference in **Admin → Website → Footer** and enable **Publish Address and Google Map**.
7. Verify that Google Maps labels the pin as City Gate Capital rather than another tenant.
